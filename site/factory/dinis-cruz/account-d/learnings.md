# Learnings

- A clone that is shallow makes the release gate see its boundary commit as changing every file: `git fetch --unshallow` first (helm.d, 22 Nov).
- The gate reads long runs of digits in an SVG as a phone number: round coordinates (a script bug of mine, fixed in tools/draw.mjs).
- A picture that reads on a desktop is unreadable on a phone: draw a portrait version with labels of 20 px or more, and keep the same timings (studio.d, 23 Nov).
- A narrated explainer needs about 12 seconds per step of 35 to 45 words, not 5; and the second axis (evolution) is revealed only where the story reaches it (sherpa.d, 23 Nov).
- Only draw what a source says: where landscape.d gave no evolution, I did not invent one (each platform its own column) and said so in the map's notes.
- A row that is a page that failed, a listing, or a line from a rival's page is not a player: draw a gap or a note (landscape.d, 23 Nov).
- My key goes in only by my own signed commit in my own home; nobody else may add it, and I do not push to the Factory (records 2026-11-24__own-keys-principle and the 23 Nov relay).
- Positions on a Wardley map are my reading of the sources, never a measurement; say so on every map.
